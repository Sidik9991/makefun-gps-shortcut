# The GPS Swears It Was a Shortcut

Entry for the make.fun bounty [The GPS Swears It Was a Shortcut](https://make.fun/bounty/u-alven-the-gps-swears-it-was-a-shortcut-2cac21d9): the GPS's own one-line excuse for sending the driver down the wrong road, at most 200 characters, no setup, no dialogue tags.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies) scores 10 fresh candidate lines against the judge's five criteria (the GPS's own excuse for the wrong road, funniest cold read, smug defensive GPS voice blaming the map, one clean breath, fresh specific detail), picks the winner, writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, and validates the 1–200 character one-line gate.

**The line (100 chars):**

> The map listed this as a shortcut with 98% confidence. The other 2% is the lake. I stand by the 98%.

Why this one: a bureaucratic machine alibi in three beats — a counted confidence figure, the lake as the entire remainder, and a closing clause that doubles down instead of apologising. The GPS blames the map, defends itself with its own logs, and the reversal lands in the last five words. No "recalculating" joke.
